# Smartup Hub: guide for clients

Smartup Hub is the Smartup support portal. Here you report a problem, follow the answer, find ready solutions and suggest ideas for the product. Button and section names below are written as the portal shows them.

## Letter to a pilot client

> Hello!
>
> We are opening Smartup Hub, the Smartup support portal, for your organization. In it you can report a problem with a screenshot, see which step your request is at and whose turn it is, find a ready solution and vote for product ideas.
>
> Portal address: {portal address}. Sign in with the same Smartup account you use in Smartup Web and the mobile app: the «Sign in with Smartup» button.

It writes no text of its own, only solutions from the base. You can call staff at any time with the «Need staff» button. You can opt out at any time by replying to this letter.

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the portal address and press «Sign in with Smartup». The Smartup sign-in page opens: sign in with the same account as in Smartup Web.
2. If your company has its own Smartup server, press «We have our own Smartup server», enter «Your Smartup address» and press «Continue». The portal remembers the server in this browser. To choose another one: «Change server».
3. On the first sign-in the portal offers to enter an email. Press «Send link» and confirm the address with the link from the email: after that you can sign in with a link sent to your inbox. If you do not need it, press «Skip»; you can add the address later in your profile.

**Signing in with an email link.** If Smartup is unavailable and your email is confirmed: on the sign-in page press «Sign in with an email link», enter the address and press «Get link». The link works for 15 minutes and only once.

**Signing in through Telegram.** If Telegram is linked, the sign-in page has the link «Open the Smartup Hub bot». The bot sends a sign-in link that works for 10 minutes.

A session ends after 30 days without visiting the portal and after 90 days in any case: then sign in again.

## 2. New request

1. On the home page press «Report a problem», or in «Requests» press «New request».
2. Fill in «What happened?»: what you did, what you expected, what happened. A document number and a warehouse name help us answer faster.
3. Attach a screenshot: the «File or screenshot» button. You can paste a screenshot from the clipboard with Ctrl+V.
4. Choose «Module» (if you are not sure, leave «Not sure») and «Impact on work»: «Work has stopped», «Seriously gets in the way», «Inconvenience» or «Question».
5. In «Who can see it» choose «My organization» so that colleagues see the request, or «Only me».
6. Press «Send request». The request number appears on the page, for example SUP-1070.

The text is kept in this browser while you write: if the connection drops, the text stays.

## 3. Solution suggestions

While you describe the problem, the portal looks for ready solutions. If a similar case was solved before, the block «Looks like this has been solved before» appears. Open the solution and try the steps. At the end of the solution answer «Did this solution help?»: «It helped» or «It did not help». If it did not help, press «Didn't help, send a request»: the request is sent with the same text.

## 4. Statuses and replies

Your requests are in «Requests». To find one, use the «Number or text» field.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| «New» | The request is received, a support agent will take it soon |
| «In progress» | A support agent is working on it |
| «Waiting for your reply» | Your answer is needed: work waits for it |
| «In development» | Smartup developers are making a fix |
| «Resolved» | The support agent considers the issue solved |
| «Closed» | The request is closed |

The request page shows whose turn it is: «Your turn» or «Support is replying now». To reply, write in the «Reply» field and press «Send». When the reply is sent, the line «Reply sent» appears.

Support agents answer during Smartup working hours: they are shown on the new request page.

## 5. Solved: confirmation and rating

When a support agent marks the request solved, press «Resolved, thanks», or «Not resolved» if the problem remains. Then rate the solution: choose a score and press «Rate». For a score of 1-3 the portal asks «What went wrong». A closed request can be opened again with «Reopen», or you can create a new one on the same topic: «Create a new one on this topic».

## 6. Notifications and Telegram

In the portal notifications always arrive: the bell in the header. Where else to send them is chosen in «Profile», block «Notifications»: «Email» and «Telegram».

To get notifications in Telegram, press «Link Telegram» in your profile. The Smartup Hub bot opens: press its start button and come back to the portal. To unlink the bot: «Unlink».

The profile also shows the devices where you are signed in (block «Sessions»). End an unknown device with «End». Ending sessions and changing the sign-in email are possible for 15 minutes after you confirm your sign-in: with a link from the email or the bot, or with «Sign in again», after which Smartup asks for your password. After an ordinary sign-in through Smartup, and later than 15 minutes, the portal asks you to «Sign in again». On a shared computer, sign out of the portal and of Smartup Web when you leave.

## 7. Support from Smartup Web

Smartup Web has the «Report a problem» button. It opens the «Smartup support» panel right inside Smartup:

- the «Report» tab: describe «What were you doing before the error?» and press «Send the request». Smartup fills in the module and the error text itself;
- the «My requests» tab: your requests and their statuses.

If the panel does not open, press «Open portal»: the same form opens in the portal.

## 8. Ideas and votes

«Ideas» collects client suggestions for the product.

1. To suggest an idea, press «+ Idea», fill in «Title» and «Description», choose «Module» if you like and press «Send idea». While you write, the portal shows similar ideas: if yours is already there, vote for it.
2. The Smartup team looks at a new idea first: status «In review». After the check it appears on the board with the status «Voting».
3. Vote with «Vote». Board tabs: «Popular», «New», «In progress», «Released».
4. Every month the Smartup team picks the idea of the month from the top five: the «Monthly selection» page. To hear about status changes of an idea, press «Follow».

Do not write phone numbers, tax numbers or other data in ideas: clients of all organizations see them.

## 9. The AI assistant Tez

Tez, the support AI assistant, replies first to a new request. Its message is signed «Tez · AI assistant» with a line saying the answer was prepared by AI from the solution base. Tez shows only published solutions: the title and steps are the same as on the solution page.

Under the answer there are three buttons: «Helped» (the request is resolved), «Did not help» and «Need staff» (the request goes to staff, Tez names the reply time). If two solutions fit, Tez asks which one is closer. Tez does not answer about payments, the contract, access rights, deleting data and other important topics: such requests go to staff at once. The same buttons are in the email and in Telegram.

## 10. Known problems and announcements

If support already knows about an error, a "We know about this problem" block appears above the new request form and in the Smartup Web support window: what is happening, the status and, if there is one, a workaround. Press "This affects me too": a request is created for you, and we will post in it as soon as the problem is fixed. Pressing again shows the number of the request already created. If your problem is different, press "I have a different problem" and describe it in the usual form.

A request linked to a known problem has a block with its status. Updates about the problem arrive in the request, by email and in Telegram, like ordinary replies. When the problem is fixed, the request moves to "Resolved": confirm that everything works or reopen the request. The list of known problems and those fixed in the last 30 days is on the "Known problems" page linked from the home page.

Support publishes planned work, outages and news as announcements: a banner above the cabinet pages, on the home page and in the support window. You can close a banner with the cross: it will not appear for you again either in the cabinet or in Smartup Web, while your colleagues still see it. Active announcements and those that ended within 90 days are on the "Announcements" page linked from the home page. During an outage Tez reports it in the request and hands the request to a specialist right away.
