# Consent to AI processing of requests in Smartup Hub

Organization: ____________________ (name, tax number ____________)

Representative: ____________________ (full name, position)

The organization agrees that requests of its employees in the Smartup Hub support portal are processed by the Smartup AI assistant.

## What this means

- The AI assistant Tez may reply to a request first: it suggests a published Smartup solution or hands the request over to a support specialist.
- The request text, its subject and attached error descriptions are sent to the external AI service Anthropic. Names, addresses and phone numbers of other people in the text are not sent if the portal recognized them.
- The AI assistant does not make decisions about rights, data or payments and does not promise fix dates.
- Anonymized request texts (without names, addresses, phone numbers and organization names) may be used by the internal Smartup support AI staff (Hermes Agent via Anthropic and OpenAI) for summaries that clients do not see.
- Servers of these providers may be located outside Uzbekistan.

## How to withdraw

The organization may withdraw its consent at any time by writing to its Smartup manager. After that, the organization's requests go straight to support staff, and AI does not process them. Requests created earlier stay in the portal for the retention period set in the data processing policy.
