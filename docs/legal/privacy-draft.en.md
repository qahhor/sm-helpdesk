# Smartup Hub personal data processing policy

Smartup Hub (hub.smartup24.com) is the support portal for Smartup clients. This page describes what data the portal stores, whom it shares it with, how long it keeps it and what rights the user has.

## What data the portal stores

- Name and, if Smartup provides them, email address and phone number.
- User identifier in Smartup, the organization and its tax number (INN).
- Telegram identifier, if the user linked the portal bot.
- Interface language.
- Request texts and their attachments.
- Technical sign-in logs: time, address the request came from, device type.

## Why the portal processes data

So that the user can sign in to the portal, create support requests and receive replies and notifications, and Smartup support can answer quickly and to the point.

## Who receives the data

The portal shares data only with providers it cannot work without (subprocessors):

- Hetzner: hosting of the portal and the database.
- Cloudflare: network, bot protection and file storage.
- Resend: sending emails.
- Telegram: notifications and backup sign-in links through the portal bot. The bot receives the request number, a subject of up to 60 characters and a link, without the request text.
- Anthropic: request texts for the first line of support and answer review, texts for solution drafts and translations.
- Hermes Agent via Anthropic and OpenAI: anonymized request texts (without names, addresses, phone numbers and organization names) for internal support summaries and analysis that clients do not see.

Servers of these providers may be located outside Uzbekistan. The list is updated in this policy before each new stage of the portal starts.

## AI assistant

The AI assistant may reply to a request first. The request text is processed by an external AI service (Anthropic). AI processing for an organization is turned on only after its written consent; without consent, requests go straight to support staff. The organization may decline AI processing at any time: contact your Smartup manager.

## How long data is kept

- Requests and correspondence: while the contract with the client is in force and 3 years after it ends.
- Attachments: 1 year after the request is closed.
- Technical logs: 30 days.
- Audit log of actions with rights and settings: 3 years.

## User rights

- Find out what data about them is stored.
- Correct the name, email address and language in the portal profile.
- Unlink Telegram in the profile at any time.
- Ask to delete the account. Then the name and email address are replaced with anonymized values, and request texts stay with the organization: it needs them for its work.

## How to contact us

Ask questions about data processing to your Smartup manager or to support through the portal.
